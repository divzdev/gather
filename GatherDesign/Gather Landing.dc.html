Ask any program lead what breaks first and they will say the chasing: bios in the wrong length, headshots that never arrive, slides named final_v3_revised_EDITED.pptx*. Gather gives every speaker a checklist and gives you one view of who still owes what.

06 · COMMUNICATIONS

Email that tells you the truth

Templates with variables, previewed against a real speaker before anything sends. Every send lands in an outbox with per-recipient delivery status, bounces requeue in one click, and calendar invites replace themselves when times change.

Step 3, always
Nothing sends on a decision click. Accept 61 talks, review the queued email, send when you say so.
Delivery truth
Delivered, queued, bounced: per recipient, with resend. "I never got it" stops being an argument.
CSV everywhere
Every list view exports what you see, filters applied. Organizers call this priceless. We agree.
