02 · Committee review

Fair by architecture.

Weighted criteria and keyboard-speed scoring. In a blind round, author identity never leaves the server. Missing reviews never count as zeros, and declared conflicts sit out of the average.

03 · Decisions

Deciding is not sending.

Record verdicts all afternoon, nothing mails. Sending is its own deliberate act, and before it happens the server recounts every recipient and refuses if your screen has gone stale.

04 · Speakers

Forty speakers, zero chasing.

Every accepted speaker gets a private checklist that works on a phone. You watch one board, sorted late-first. Reminders space themselves, and calendar invites update when the schedule moves: Google, Outlook, iCal.

05 · Schedule

06 · Publish

One press. Every surface.

Publishing cuts a numbered snapshot. The public schedule, talk pages, speaker directory and your own website's embed all read that same snapshot. A half-finished edit can never leak, and any earlier version restores in one move.
